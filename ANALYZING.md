# ANALYZING.md — how to analyze a varnishgather

The detailed guide for analyzing a varnishgather has moved to a
Claude Code skill at `skills/analyze-varnishgather/`.

Start with [SKILL.md](skills/analyze-varnishgather/SKILL.md).  You'll
also find helper scripts at `skills/analyze-varnishgather/scripts/`
(`vg-check` for automated warning detection and `vg-log` for reading the
raw VSL log), and detailed references in
`skills/analyze-varnishgather/references/`.

For installation and update instructions, see the "Analyzing a gather with
Claude Code" section of `README.rst`.

The official Varnish Enterprise troubleshooting guide at
https://docs.varnish-software.com/varnish-enterprise/troubleshooting/
remains an authoritative companion reference — it describes known issues,
their metrics/symptoms, and concrete remediation steps.
